# Research-session entry contract

Before research or implementation work, read `spectral-krylov-jepa/research/RESEARCH_STATE.json` and the applicable historical truth and protocol records it references. This state indexes existing history; it does not replace it.

Before finishing, append actual decisions, verification, failures, unresolved gates, and the next action. Preserve negative, invalid, and inconclusive outcomes and their originating versions. Engineering tests do not establish efficacy or complete scientific checkpoints.

Keep protected evaluation protected. A material change to a hypothesis, mechanism, evaluation, or claim requires a recorded development version or successor study. Completed predecessor evidence remains intact.
